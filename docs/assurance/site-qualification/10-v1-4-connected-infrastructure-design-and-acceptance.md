## Acceptance is a dependency graph, not a numbered permission ladder

G3 production activation requires accepted G0/G1/G2 prerequisites, the applicable G4 operational and recovery readiness, current per-workload evidence and valid authority to operate. G4 also covers subsequent recurring exercises and continuing acceptance; those later activities cannot defer initial proof of a promised recovery service. Restricted qualification fixtures use a separate approved non-production scope and can exist before G2 to generate its evidence. The gate IDs remain stable for traceability. Record the scope and evidence consumed by each gate rather than assuming numeric order authorizes an incomplete service.

Authoritative linked treatment: [WD — Worked Infrastructure Design, Build Schedules and Acceptance](../../solutions/internal-protected-workload/README.md#V14_WD_START)

Read this clarification with the original chapter. It does not create an exemption to an adopted requirement, waive a mandatory control, select an unknown site value or report a live test as passed.


<a id="source-table-118"></a>

| Acceptance scope | Required predecessors | Not equivalent to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restricted qualification fixture | Approved non-production scope; accepted foundational access; installed candidate platform and necessary safeguards. | Production G3, qualified service advertising or permission for production data. |
| Production G3 | Applicable G0/G1/G2; current G4 readiness for the promised service; workload-specific evidence and valid authority. | Permission to defer owner, restore, key custody, support or required recovery decisions until later. |
| Continuing G4 | Accepted initial readiness plus recurring and change-triggered evidence within approved scope. | A retroactive substitute for unproven initial service commitments. |
