# P11 — Pilot and supported release

Commission a bounded production pilot and release a service that a receiving team can operate and recover.

**Accountable lead:** Product and service owner. Individual assignees and reviewers are recorded when assigned.

**Entry:** G10 acceptance and separate production authority; all pilot scope, participants and observation criteria agreed.

**Sequence:** P11.01 → P11.02 → P11.03 → P11.04; P11.05 resolves retained history before any separate retirement.

Use the [programme plan](../phased-plan.md), [canonical delivery register](../delivery-register.yaml), [requirements](../requirements-and-qualification.md) and [decision register](../../decisions/decision-register.md) together. The register owns current state and evidence; these cards define delivery scope. Refine staffing and estimates using the [estimation model](../estimation-and-dependencies.md) before starting each package.

## Work packages

### P11.01 — Production commissioning

**Owner:** SRE/service owners. **Traceability:** R35; G11.01.

**Dependencies and inputs:** G10 acceptance for selected release and production change authorization; named environment/service owners.

**Outputs:** Production readiness record for environment, tenant/site scope, identities, backups, monitoring and windows.

1. Compare installed candidate/configuration with qualified artifact/tuple scope and approved trust boundary.
2. Verify scoped credentials, restore/stop readiness and receiving owner coverage before native pilot enablement.

**Acceptance:** Unqualified drift or missing authority blocks commissioning; production enablement has a distinct authorized scope.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G11.01. Reviewer: SRE/security/service owners.

### P11.02 — Controlled pilot

**Owner:** Product/operators. **Traceability:** R33 R35; G11.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P11.01 commissioning and agreed participant, dataset, limits, observation period and stop criteria.

**Outputs:** Controlled first tenant/application pilot, operator training material and Q10 observation record.

1. Train operators on plan review, holds, recovery and evidence; rehearse tasks before bounded native execution.
2. Observe integrity, usability, performance and service outcomes for the agreed period; route defects to owning packages.

**Acceptance:** Independent operators complete authorized tasks and identify unsafe/held conditions; early success does not shorten agreed observation silently.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G11.02. Reviewer: Product/application and service owners.

### P11.03 — Acceptance

**Owner:** Application/security/service owners. **Traceability:** R33 R35; G11.02 G11.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P11.02 pilot observations, current runbooks and receiving support/escalation owners.

**Outputs:** Functional, security and service acceptance record with observed operational handover.

1. Have an operator other than the developer install/recover in the approved recovery environment using released runbooks.
2. Exercise support escalation and audit export; document remaining limitations and explicit acceptance decisions.

**Acceptance:** Receiving team can operate the bounded service without developer-only knowledge or expanded unauthorized credentials.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G11.02 G11.03. Reviewer: Receiving operations and qualification leads.

### P11.04 — Release publication

**Owner:** Engineering/SRE. **Traceability:** R35; G11.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P11.03 acceptance and final candidate identity unchanged or affected qualification rerun.

**Outputs:** Signed release manifest, exact support matrix, install/upgrade guides, limitations and support ownership.

1. Reconcile source/image/lock/SBOM/signature identities against evidence and installed scope.
2. Publish bounded release notes and operational contacts, retention/retest rules and upgrade constraints.

**Acceptance:** Release artifacts and every support claim resolve to the accepted revisions; untested tuples remain excluded.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G11.04. Reviewer: Product/service owners.

### P11.05 — Historical disposition

**Owner:** Product/records owner. **Traceability:** R36; G11.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** ADR-021 applicability review, records/retention requirements and P10.05 import evidence where applicable.

**Outputs:** Reviewed historical disposition decision, archive/import verification and separate retirement authorization.

1. Identify any retained operational intent/evidence and decide archive, import or reviewed non-applicability.
2. Preserve originals, reconcile required records and confirm old-writer disposition before separately authorized retirement.

**Acceptance:** No previous branch or operational data is deleted incidentally; applicable retention and integrity requirements have accountable disposition.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G11.04. Reviewer: Product/service/records owners.

## Review and handoff

Use campaign designs [Q10](../../qualification/campaigns/q10-scale-and-pilot.md) for the applicable scope; use [canonical G11 criteria](../gates.md) for required evidence levels and environments. Review through the [G11 procedure](../../qualification/gate-reviews/g11.md).

Record unavailable inputs with owner, unblock condition and independent work still possible. A failed check becomes corrective work against its owning package; narrowing scope requires review and corresponding support-matrix changes.

Update the owning service/contract, operating procedure and evidence references with the change. Keep actual gate decisions in the delivery register, regenerate progress/traceability, and update `next_work.md` with the next executable action. Use the [work-package template](../../templates/work-package.md) when splitting a package into implementation tasks.
