## Activation prerequisite correction and connected build receipts

<!-- SOURCE-BLOCK PROV:100 END -->

<!-- SOURCE-BLOCK PROV:101 BEGIN -->

Use WD §§9–10 for the gate dependency and owned-resource build schedule. Installation of candidate infrastructure and restricted qualification fixtures does not equal G2 acceptance. Production G3 requires the applicable G4 readiness evidence before activation, including promised recovery capability, owners, custody, support and authority to operate. A later recurring G4 exercise does not replace that initial requirement. Final handover confirms the accepted as-built scope; it must not first discover a missing owner or protection obligation after production exposure.
