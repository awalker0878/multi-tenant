# P04 — Site commissioning and inventory

Commission a read-only trust path and expose complete, attributable inventory while retaining ownership ambiguity.

**Accountable lead:** Infrastructure engineering lead. Individual assignees and reviewers are recorded when assigned.

**Entry:** G01 runtime and P02 trust/tenant interfaces; owners authorize selected OpenStack and VMware read scopes.

**Sequence:** P04.01 → P04.02 → P04.03; P04.04/P04.05 use stable collector and observation contracts.

Use the [programme plan](../phased-plan.md), [canonical delivery register](../delivery-register.yaml), [requirements](../requirements-and-qualification.md) and [decision register](../../decisions/decision-register.md) together. The register owns current state and evidence; these cards define delivery scope. Refine staffing and estimates using the [estimation model](../estimation-and-dependencies.md) before starting each package.

## Work packages

### P04.01 — Site enrollment

**Owner:** Inventory/SRE. **Traceability:** R08 R10; G04.02 G04.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P01.06 trust controls, P02.03 authorization and ADR-011/015 site interfaces.

**Outputs:** Site/endpoint records, worker enrollment/revocation APIs and read-only commissioning procedure.

1. Bind approved site owner, endpoint allowlist, worker identity and secret references at enrollment.
2. Exercise trust renewal and revocation; distinguish registration, read-only commissioning and later native write commissioning.

**Acceptance:** An unapproved endpoint or revoked worker cannot collect; enrollment creates no native write entitlement.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G04.02 G04.04. Reviewer: Inventory/platform owners and security reviewer.

### P04.02 — Collectors

**Owner:** Inventory. **Traceability:** R08 R09 R10 R13; G04.01 G04.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P04.01 enrolled lab endpoints; accepted read scopes and installed tuple identities from ADR-015.

**Outputs:** OpenStack and VMware read-only collectors, normalized profile facts and all-three-platform contract coverage.

1. Collect stable native IDs, installed features, API/backend versions and observation provenance without administrator scope.
2. Declare unknown/unsupported dimensions explicitly, including AHV contract gaps; bound pagination and privilege assumptions.

**Acceptance:** Native rediscovery preserves identity; hidden permission gaps remain visible and no omitted capability becomes an implied supported fact.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G04.01 G04.02. Reviewer: Platform owners and qualification lead.

### P04.03 — Observation store

**Owner:** Inventory. **Traceability:** R08 R09 R10 R11; G04.02 G04.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P04.02 observation contracts and inventory ownership rules; ADR-013 resource-reference model.

**Outputs:** Generation/snapshot/delta model, freshness/tombstone rules and ownership-collision reports.

1. Preserve collected-at, source scope, completion and expiry independently from product desired intent.
2. Reconcile complete generations before inferring deletion; retain reused-ID ambiguity and old/new identity history.

**Acceptance:** Partial pages cannot masquerade as complete inventory; reused IDs or ownership collisions hold matching/adoption; expiry removes readiness claims.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G04.02 G04.04. Reviewer: Inventory owner and independent platform observer.

### P04.04 — Discovery controls

**Owner:** Infrastructure. **Traceability:** R04 R10 R34; G04.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P04.02 collectors, approved endpoint quotas and P00.05 initial workload tiers.

**Outputs:** Discovery scheduler limits, per-tenant budgets, bounded retry/backpressure and metrics.

1. Apply rate and concurrency limits per tenant and authoritative endpoint, including shared endpoint budgets.
2. Resume interrupted pagination without false completeness; measure fairness and reconnect behavior under controlled load.

**Acceptance:** Competing tenants remain within budgets; disconnected sites become stale; recovery reconciles a fresh generation before readiness resumes.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G04.03. Reviewer: SRE and inventory owners.

### P04.05 — Inventory experience

**Owner:** Console. **Traceability:** R10 R33; G04.02 G04.03 G04.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P04.03 freshness/ownership semantics and P04.04 health metrics; P02.05 access controls.

**Outputs:** Site health, inventory browse and application-resource matching review screens.

1. Expose source scope, version, collected-at and incomplete/stale reasons alongside observed resources.
2. Present proposed matches for operator review without granting ownership or obscuring collisions.

**Acceptance:** Operators distinguish observed capacity from reserved capacity and read-only registration from write readiness; tenant-scoped searches do not leak facts.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G04.02 G04.03 G04.04. Reviewer: Product and inventory owners.

## Engineering acceptance detail

P04.01/P04.04 apply [the outbound and hostile-input security rules](../../engineering/security-and-tenancy.md): endpoint registration establishes an approved site-specific destination, protocol and credential scope; supplied URLs, redirects and resolved addresses cannot broaden it. Approved private infrastructure remains reachable through that explicit boundary. Bound pages, response size, decompression, parsing and connector concurrency, and test denied egress independently of application validation.

P04.03/P04.05 apply tenant-aware query/cache limits and [safe frontend data handling](../../engineering/frontend.md) to search, discovery summaries and exports. Missing or partial observations must not become an empty successful result. Attach negative cases to G04.02/G04.03 and Q02.
