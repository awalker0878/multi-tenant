# P07 — Native OpenStack provisioning

Provision, activate, recover and retire one native OpenStack application within exactly authorized and independently observed scope.

**Accountable lead:** Infrastructure and platform owners. Individual assignees and reviewers are recorded when assigned.

**Entry:** G06 closed, P01 operating controls present and scoped native lab approved; selected tuple matches ADR-014/015.

**Sequence:** P07.01 → P07.02/P07.03 → P07.04 → P07.05/P07.06; reservations are native effects subject to authority checks.

The [preparation implementation](../p07-native-provisioning.md) and
[commissioning runbook](../../operations/runbooks/openstack-commissioning.md)
provide bounded input and saved-plan comparison commands. The
[native adapter components](../../operations/runbooks/openstack-native-adapters.md)
add actual protected-byte inspection, independent OpenStack readback, durable
claims and quarantined Terraform modules, with E2 component verification. The
[remaining implementation/review packet](../p07-completion-review.md) identifies
actual missing inputs and native outputs; these tools do not complete this phase.

Use the [programme plan](../phased-plan.md), [canonical delivery register](../delivery-register.yaml), [requirements](../requirements-and-qualification.md) and [decision register](../../decisions/decision-register.md) together. The register owns current state and evidence; these cards define delivery scope. Refine staffing and estimates using the [estimation model](../estimation-and-dependencies.md) before starting each package.

## Work packages

### P07.01 — Native site readiness

**Owner:** SRE/platform owners. **Traceability:** R08 R18 R31; G07.01 G07.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** G06 closed, P01 baseline operating controls and ADR-015 exact OpenStack tuple; separately authorized lab.

**Outputs:** Native site readiness checklist and scoped write commissioning record.

1. Verify endpoint allowlists, credentials, network/storage baselines, quotas, backup restore prerequisites and receiving owners.
2. Rehearse stop/revocation before provisioning and bind the campaign to approved resources, datasets and impact.

**Acceptance:** No native write starts with missing readiness or scope; withdrawal of authority prevents new privileged boundaries.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G07.01 G07.02. Reviewer: Platform/service owners and security lead.

### P07.02 — Infrastructure automation

**Owner:** Infrastructure. **Traceability:** R07 R16 R18 R19; G07.01 G07.02 G07.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P07.01 commissioning and ADR-016 saved-plan/backend/resource-field ownership.

**Outputs:** OpenStack adapters, Terraform modules and reviewed saved-plan apply/readback contracts.

1. Pin tool/provider/module/backend/workspace and target tuple identities in the immutable plan.
2. Assign one writer per managed field, validate saved-plan binding before apply and reconcile uncertain acceptance through independent readback.

**Acceptance:** Wrong plan, workspace, scope or ownership fails before mutation; lost response does not cause duplicate allocation.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G07.01 G07.02 G07.03. Reviewer: Infrastructure and independent platform reviewers.

### P07.03 — Guest and service integration

**Owner:** Infrastructure/service owners. **Traceability:** R17 R18 R19 R21; G07.01 G07.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P07.02 infrastructure, P05.03 reservation contracts and approved image/service interfaces from ADR-015.

**Outputs:** Native reservation receipts, guest hardening and IPAM/DNS/identity/time/logging/monitoring/backup integrations.

1. Acquire scoped allocations as approved effects, journal attempts and owner receipts, then revalidate dependent prerequisites.
2. Apply guest configuration and enroll services through named authoritative interfaces; test usable services and restore.

**Acceptance:** Partial owner success reconciles or compensates safely; correct addresses/identities and actual backup restore are independently observed.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G07.01 G07.03. Reviewer: Guest/service owners and qualification lead.

### P07.04 — Activation and verification

**Owner:** Lifecycle/quality. **Traceability:** R07 R18 R19 R20 R21 R33; G07.01 G07.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P07.02/P07.03 preparation and accepted policy/service expected-outcome matrix.

**Outputs:** Quarantine-to-active workflow, independent native readiness and Q05/Q06 verification.

1. Observe intended object mappings, hardening, service paths and application health before traffic activation.
2. Test positive and negative paths across same-host/subnet, inter-host and edge boundaries with relevant IPv6/return cases.

**Acceptance:** Failed mandatory readiness or isolation holds activation; healthy status requires service observations beyond successful automation exit.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G07.01 G07.02. Reviewer: Security/application owners and independent observer.

### P07.05 — Failure and retirement

**Owner:** Lifecycle. **Traceability:** R16 R25 R29; G07.03 G07.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P07.04 active/held states, P06.03 operation journal and approved retention/deletion authority.

**Outputs:** Partial-provision recovery, drift hold and separately approved retirement workflow.

1. Inject restart, revocation, response loss and activation failures; reconcile owned objects and reservations before retry.
2. Retire only explicitly authorized objects/allocations after retention checks, independently confirming deletion and release.

**Acceptance:** Unknown outcomes retain holds; retained keys/data remain available; unowned objects and field scopes are untouched.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G07.03 G07.04. Reviewer: Lifecycle/governance and qualification leads.

### P07.06 — Native support dossier

**Owner:** Assurance. **Traceability:** R18 R20 R30; G07.01 G07.02 G07.03 G07.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P07.01–P07.05 evidence and exact tested artifact/tuple identities.

**Outputs:** Native provisioning support dossier, reviewed limits, runbook references and measured recovery timings.

1. Assemble Q04/Q05/Q06 positive, negative, failure and restore records by exact capability claim.
2. Review evidence compatibility/expiry and publish only supported operations with explicit unknown/unsupported limitations.

**Acceptance:** Each published row resolves to reviewed native evidence at the same tuple/revision; receiving operators can identify scope and recovery limits.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G07.01 G07.02 G07.03 G07.04. Reviewer: Assurance and service owners.

Update the owning service/contract, operating procedure and evidence references with the change. Keep actual gate decisions in the delivery register, regenerate progress/traceability, and update `next_work.md` with the next executable action.
