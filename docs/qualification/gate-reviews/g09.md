# G09 review — Platform and capability expansion

This procedure applies the [canonical G09 criteria](../../implementation/gates.md) to the scope delivered by [P09](../../implementation/phases/p09.md). Use the [shared review process](README.md); the [delivery register](../../implementation/delivery-register.yaml) owns actual evidence references, reviewer identities and the gate decision.

**Review roles:** Product, architecture, platform, security, inventory/lifecycle, SRE and qualification reviewers.

**Environment:** A named expansion tranche with separately authorized exact-tuple labs; evaluate each advertised direction, method, guest and topology individually.

## Assemble the review packet

Tranche baseline, Q08 and applicable Q02/Q04/Q05/Q06/Q07/Q10 run manifests, adapter conformance and upgrade/recovery records.

Confirm the source/artifact identities, selected scope, authorization references, independent observation and evidence validity before judging results. Resolve missing artifacts as explicit review gaps.

## Review in this order

1. **G09.01:** Freeze included operations, directions, guest/method/topology variants and exclusions. Reconcile R26–R28 and other applicable obligations to concrete tranche packages and campaigns.
2. **G09.02:** Review native positive, negative and recovery results for each advertised tuple. Reject vendor-wide, reverse-direction or different-method inference; preserve unsupported and unknown rows.
3. **G09.03:** For adoption, inspect no-change import, exact object/field ownership and independent old-writer transfer observations before mutation. Review collision/drift holds and detach/reconciliation behavior.
4. **G09.04:** Check impact analysis and affected common-path reruns. Inspect adapter upgrade/recovery, concurrent risk budgets and scheduling pause/stop; verify operator runbooks reflect the qualified limitations.

## Stop and corrective work

A tranche cannot close with any unsupported advertised tuple, inherited adoption authority or missing affected-path rerun. Narrowing the tranche requires explicit scope review.

Retain failed/missing cases and assign corrective work to the owning package with an unblock condition. A changed supported scope must be reflected in requirements coverage, the support matrix and the run manifest before another decision.

## Record the decision

Record an assessment for each G09 criterion, immutable evidence IDs and limitations, actual reviewer/date, blockers and the reviewed scope. Apply [status rules](../../implementation/status-model.md); a review document or a passing subset does not close the gate.

Identify material changes that require reruns, update the register, regenerate derived progress/traceability and state the next executable action. This gate review does not itself authorize unrelated native or production changes.
