## OPS-001

Each service SHALL publish a provider/tenant/shared control responsibility matrix and accountable decision owners for its complete lifecycle, including inherited controls, support access and data disposal.


### OPS-001.A01

**Assertion:** Complete source obligation; all applicable clauses must be satisfied: Each service SHALL publish a provider/tenant/shared control responsibility matrix and accountable decision owners for its complete lifecycle, including inherited controls, support access and data disposal.

**Owner / location:** Service management / Actual operational service, independent telemetry, capacity/failure domains and lifecycle authorities
