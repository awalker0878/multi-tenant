# P06 — Durable execution in simulation

Exercise the entire durable control path under simulated faults before native mutations enter scope.

**Accountable lead:** Infrastructure engineering lead. Individual assignees and reviewers are recorded when assigned.

**Entry:** P02 authority and P05 plans; P01 persistence/observability/recovery controls; ADR-007/018/020 resolved for execution.

**Sequence:** P06.01 → P06.02/P06.03/P06.04 → P06.05; P06.06 consumes reviewed state and evidence contracts.

Use the [programme plan](../phased-plan.md), [canonical delivery register](../delivery-register.yaml), [requirements](../requirements-and-qualification.md) and [decision register](../../decisions/decision-register.md) together. The register owns current state and evidence; these cards define delivery scope. Refine staffing and estimates using the [estimation model](../estimation-and-dependencies.md) before starting each package.

## Work packages

### P06.01 — Admission and dispatch

**Owner:** Lifecycle. **Traceability:** R03 R13 R14 R15; G06.01 G06.02 G06.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P05.06 admission contract, P02.04 approvals and P01.03 outbox; ADR-007/018 semantics.

**Outputs:** Lifecycle admission transaction, stable workflow ID and outbox-to-Temporal dispatch.

1. Commit admission and dispatch intent atomically with tenant-scoped idempotency and payload conflict checks.
2. Start or recover one logical workflow on duplicate delivery; validate isolated campaign endpoint and authority scope separately.

**Acceptance:** Lost start response or repeated command does not duplicate workflows; unsupported operational tuples and stale/revoked authority are denied.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G06.01 G06.02 G06.03. Reviewer: Lifecycle/security owners and quality lead.

### P06.02 — Workflow state

**Owner:** Lifecycle. **Traceability:** R15 R22 R23 R24 R29; G06.01 G06.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P06.01 admission records and approved ADR-007 workflow evolution/timeout model.

**Outputs:** Provision, migrate, recover and retire workflow states, activity contracts and operator hold transitions.

1. Define persisted transitions, safe cancellation points and bounded retry rules by effect certainty.
2. Separate workflow progress from native authority and journal ownership; model pre-target-write rollback and post-write recovery explicitly.

**Acceptance:** Restart resumes authorized work; timeout after possible acceptance enters held reconciliation instead of blind replay; cancellation never asserts an effect was undone.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G06.01 G06.03. Reviewer: Lifecycle and SRE leads.

### P06.03 — Execution authority

**Owner:** Lifecycle/workers. **Traceability:** R03 R04 R16 R17 R23 R31; G06.02 G06.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P06.02 activity boundaries, P05.03 journal/receipt contracts and ADR-009/016/020 authority choices.

**Outputs:** Worker grant, lease/epoch, field-ownership and operation-journal protocol.

1. Recheck plan, approval, worker, resource scope and the applicable operational-qualification or isolated-campaign authority immediately before a privileged effect.
2. Persist attempt identity and native correlation keys; keep resource holds when lease expiry or lost acknowledgement leaves outcome unknown.

**Acceptance:** A resumed stale worker cannot claim current authority; duplicate attempt reconciles native readback; no retry/release follows uncertainty without resolution.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G06.02 G06.03. Reviewer: Security, lifecycle and independent quality reviewers.

### P06.04 — Evidence custody

**Owner:** Assurance. **Traceability:** R13 R31; G06.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P01.05 protected storage, ADR-010 custody and P06.01 operation/plan identifiers.

**Outputs:** Assurance upload/finalize APIs, digest/metadata bindings, access and qualification review records.

1. Finalize evidence only after digest verification and bind it to scope, source revision, operation and independent observation.
2. Apply retention, redaction and tenant access; keep E2 simulation distinct from E3 exact-tuple native evidence.

**Acceptance:** Tampered digest, wrong tenant and incompatible scope fail review; an unexecuted design cannot advance a support claim.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G06.04. Reviewer: Assurance/security owners.

### P06.05 — Simulation and fault injection

**Owner:** Quality. **Traceability:** R15 R16 R17 R22 R23 R24 R29; G06.01 G06.02 G06.03 G06.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P06.01–P06.04 integrated path, Q01/Q03/Q04 cases and real persistence/Temporal dependencies.

**Outputs:** Deterministic simulated adapters and fault campaign timelines.

1. Inject crash, duplicate dispatch, delayed acceptance, lost response, revocation and partition around each important effect boundary.
2. Observe operation journal and independent simulated state; check reservations and source/target writer authority under failure.

**Acceptance:** One logical effect or explicit held uncertainty remains; restart, recovery and emergency stop preserve authority and evidence bindings.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G06.01 G06.02 G06.03 G06.04. Reviewer: Quality lead independent of workflow author.

### P06.06 — Jobs experience

**Owner:** Console. **Traceability:** R30 R33; G06.01 G06.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P06.02 workflow projections and P06.04 evidence access; P02 permission model.

**Outputs:** Jobs timeline, held/unknown explanations, cancel requests and authorized recovery actions.

1. Expose authoritative state freshness, pending requests and evidence links with simulation labeling.
2. Require scoped authority and current state for recovery; show when an operator decision remains necessary.

**Acceptance:** The full simulated journey is understandable through the console; stale UI or duplicate cancellation cannot grant a new effect.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G06.01 G06.04. Reviewer: Product and qualification leads.

## Engineering acceptance detail

P06.01/P06.02 preserve the [queue versus workflow boundary](../../engineering/data-and-messaging.md): Laravel background jobs perform bounded owning-service work; lifecycle/Temporal retains durable native orchestration and operation authority. P06.05 covers driver-specific redelivery/timeouts and duplicate messages alongside real Temporal replay and unknown-effect tests. Neither a framework fake nor replay alone proves effective fencing or native outcomes.

P06.04 continues authorized custody/audit processing for already committed facts when an initiating user's grant is later revoked; new effects and evidence retrieval still require current authority. P06.06 follows [frontend rules](../../engineering/frontend.md) for bounded polling, reconnect, stale revisions, history cleanup and accessible held/cancelled states. Extend Q04 and the browser journey evidence under G06.01/G06.03/G06.04.
