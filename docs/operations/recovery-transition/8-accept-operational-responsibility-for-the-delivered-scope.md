Initial G4 operational and recovery readiness precedes G3 production activation. The later as-built confirmation records what was activated and handed over; it must not be the first occasion on which support or recovery ownership is discovered. Continuing exercises confirm that the service remains inside its accepted envelope.

<!-- SOURCE-BLOCK OPS:91 END -->

<!-- SOURCE-BLOCK OPS:92 BEGIN -->

A material change can invalidate only part of a qualification or a wider inherited dependency. Record the affected scope, conditions for existing operation, restrictions on new placement and the evidence required for reacceptance. Keep historical authorization and actual current technical status separately attributable.

<!-- SOURCE-BLOCK OPS:92 END -->

Current release status: developed operational procedures and editable records supplied. No site acceptance, real recovery measurement, supplier approval or operating authorization is asserted.
