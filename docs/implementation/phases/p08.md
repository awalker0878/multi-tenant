# P08 — VMware-to-OpenStack migration

Implement the [P08 native migration design](../p08-native-migration.md), including capability profiles, explicit method selection, isolated clone/export, copy-only conversion and guest transformation, native target import, delta strategies and separate recovery boundaries.

Current delivered components and remaining obligations are recorded in the [implementation record](../p08-execution.md) and [completion packet](../p08-completion-review.md). The [component custody runbook](../../operations/runbooks/native-migration.md) preserves the native qualification boundary.

The [profile integration evidence](../../../verification/p08/profiles/qualification-index.json)
now covers leased Inventory collection/persistence, Console confirmation, authenticated
Planning preparation and exact current-profile admission. These delivered interfaces
still require actual endpoint/owner commissioning, complete native plan/stage composition
and the selected application/guest/service protocols before a native Q07 journey.

**Accountable lead:** Infrastructure and application owner. Individual assignees and reviewers are recorded when assigned.

**Entry:** G07 path qualified; ADR-014 method selected and all source, application and service owners available.

**Sequence:** P08.01 → P08.02 → P08.03 → P08.04; P08.05 is designed before cutover and proved before P08.06.

Use the [programme plan](../phased-plan.md), [canonical delivery register](../delivery-register.yaml), [requirements](../requirements-and-qualification.md) and [decision register](../../decisions/decision-register.md) together. The register owns current state and evidence; these cards define delivery scope. Refine staffing and estimates using the [estimation model](../estimation-and-dependencies.md) before starting each package.

## Work packages

### P08.01 — Source readiness

**Owner:** Inventory/lifecycle. **Traceability:** R22; G08.01 G08.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** G07 provisioning path and ADR-014 explicitly selected migration method; current VMware read-only observations.

**Outputs:** API-discovered source machine list, named migration groups and bulk OpenStack selection; normalized SourceWorkloadProfile and TargetCapabilityProfile, all-disk/dataset inventory and per-VM migration readiness assessment.

1. Map workload membership, dependencies, native IDs, source datasets, mounts, NICs and keys to approved source scope. Verify all disk/backing chains, native export/import support, firmware, controllers, guest drivers, encryption and secret/identity replacement rules.
2. Confirm capture/export authority, consistency groups and required service/identity preservation with the application owner.

**Acceptance:** Every approved dataset and dependency is accounted for; ambiguous ownership or incompatible features block a migration plan.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G08.01 G08.02. Reviewer: Application/platform owners and qualification lead.

### P08.02 — Method and data movement

**Owner:** Infrastructure. **Traceability:** R17 R22 R31; G08.01.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P08.01 readiness, P05.03 budget contracts and approved direct data-flow endpoints.

**Outputs:** Native ExportVm/NFC and destination import implementation with durable uncertainty holds, complete disk mapping and checksum verification.

1. Pin native API/adapter and conversion artifacts, encryption/key references and bounded transfer, staging and retained-source demand. Capture consistent S0 and an isolated powered-off exact-S0 clone; qualify clone/network isolation before export.
2. Export the clone using ExportVm/NFC and OVF/manifest mappings; move bounded payloads through approved endpoints. Convert/transform only the copy, import with the selected native target APIs and verify all digests/metadata. Delta methods require their own final synchronization and consistency receipts.

**Acceptance:** No payload crosses console/event bus; interrupted transfer holds custody until an approved reconciliation; evidence names only the selected method and tested guest/data profile.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G08.01. Reviewer: Infrastructure/security and application reviewers.

### P08.03 — Rehearsal

**Owner:** Lifecycle/application owner. **Traceability:** R22 R23; G08.01 G08.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P08.02 movement and isolated representative dataset; signed-off correctness and outage measures.

**Outputs:** Rehearsal workflow and measured application validation/cleanup dossier.

1. Build an isolated target with suppressed business effects and blocked production writer paths.
2. Run selected method, verify all datasets/services/policy and record capture, transfer, recovery and total downtime components.

**Acceptance:** Rehearsal cannot send production business effects; independent application checks establish correctness and cleanup preserves required evidence.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G08.01 G08.04. Reviewer: Application and qualification owners.

### P08.04 — Cutover

**Owner:** Lifecycle/governance. **Traceability:** R21 R23 R33; G08.02 G08.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** Successful scoped rehearsal review, current plan/approval and approved change window; source fencing mechanism.

**Outputs:** Ordered cutover workflow with source/other-writer fencing, final synchronization and controlled traffic activation.

1. Revalidate actor, plan, scope, reservations and operational qualification or separately scoped lab campaign authority; independently observe writer fencing before final transfer.
2. Check target integrity and required services, then authorize traffic/writes at the recorded transition boundary.

**Acceptance:** Unfenced source, stale authority or failed final integrity blocks activation; timeout enters held recovery and never silently retries cutover.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G08.02 G08.04. Reviewer: Security/application owners and independent observer.

### P08.05 — Recovery decisions

**Owner:** Infrastructure/application owner. **Traceability:** R24; G08.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P08.04 explicit first-target-write boundary and owner-approved divergence/recovery objectives.

**Outputs:** Pre-write rollback and post-write source-return or forward-recovery procedures with operator decision states.

1. Exercise interruption before target writes and prove source restoration remains safe.
2. Create accepted target changes, interrupt service and execute the selected reconciliation/forward recovery without discarding those changes.

**Acceptance:** Recovery proves bounded data/outage objectives and one writer; restarting an old source without reconciliation is never treated as post-write rollback.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G08.03. Reviewer: Application/SRE and independent qualification reviewers.

### P08.06 — Acceptance

**Owner:** Quality/assurance. **Traceability:** R20 R21 R22 R23 R24 R25; G08.01 G08.02 G08.03 G08.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P08.03–P08.05 observed results plus Q05/Q06 service and policy evidence.

**Outputs:** Q07 migration dossier, bounded support row and reviewed source retention/retirement conditions.

1. Reconcile timestamps, all-dataset checks, security paths, method/tuple/artifact scope and independent recovery evidence.
2. Document residual constraints and separate source-deletion authority after acceptance/retention; leave reverse direction unqualified.

**Acceptance:** G08 criteria resolve to compatible native evidence; actual measured outcomes meet the selected owner-approved limits.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G08.01 G08.02 G08.03 G08.04. Reviewer: Application/service owners and assurance.
