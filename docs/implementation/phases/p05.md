# P05 — Capabilities and immutable plans

Compile explainable destination assessments and immutable plans from versioned intent, trustworthy facts and current qualification.

**Accountable lead:** Infrastructure engineering lead. Individual assignees and reviewers are recorded when assigned.

**Entry:** P03 intent, P04 native observations and agreed policy baseline; ADR-016 plan ownership decided before compilation.

**Sequence:** P05.01 → P05.02 → P05.03/P05.04 → P05.05/P05.06; lifecycle owns reservation authority.

Use the [programme plan](../phased-plan.md), [canonical delivery register](../delivery-register.yaml), [requirements](../requirements-and-qualification.md) and [decision register](../../decisions/decision-register.md) together. The register owns current state and evidence; these cards define delivery scope. Refine staffing and estimates using the [estimation model](../estimation-and-dependencies.md) before starting each package.

## Work packages

### P05.01 — Capability registry

**Owner:** Planning. **Traceability:** R12 R13; G05.01 G05.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P04.02 profile facts and P00/ADR-015 installed tuple vocabulary; assurance qualification contract.

**Outputs:** Versioned capability dimensions, adapter declarations and support-evidence resolution contract.

1. Define operation, method, constraint and version semantics across VMware, AHV and OpenStack.
2. Keep declarations, observations and qualified support separate; retain unknown and unsupported rows for every required dimension.

**Acceptance:** Profile completeness never implies support; unsupported or stale mandatory dimensions produce a named assessment blocker.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G05.01 G05.03. Reviewer: Planning/assurance owners.

### P05.02 — Policy and assessment

**Owner:** Planning. **Traceability:** R04 R06 R07 R08 R11 R12 R13 R19 R20 R21 R32; G05.01 G05.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P03.02 requirement revisions, P04.03 observations and P05.01 capabilities; ADR-013/015/017 constraints.

**Outputs:** Deterministic assessment rules for policy, domain placement, shared services, location and sovereignty.

1. Resolve pinned requirement/policy/profile versions against exact destination facts and qualification.
2. Return eligible, conditional, blocked or unknown with requirement-specific reasons and remediation; do not rank whole vendor families.

**Acceptance:** Two authorized destinations yield explainable outcomes; missing mandatory isolation, service or custody evidence prevents operational eligibility.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G05.01 G05.03. Reviewer: Planning, security and assurance owners.

### P05.03 — Capacity and reservations

**Owner:** Planning/lifecycle. **Traceability:** R17; G05.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P05.02 demand and P00.02 ownership; lifecycle/service-owner reservation interfaces.

**Outputs:** Reservation intent/receipt schemas and lifecycle journal design with simulated reserve, renew, confirm and release.

1. Model atomic local intent separately from authoritative owner allocation and capacity observations.
2. Simulate competing demand, partial success, timeout and expiry; reconcile or compensate known receipts without allocating native resources.

**Acceptance:** No oversubscription is inferred from cached capacity; timeout holds uncertainty; expiry alone never releases observed live resources.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G05.04. Reviewer: Lifecycle and quality leads.

### P05.04 — Plan compilation

**Owner:** Planning. **Traceability:** R14 R19; G05.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P05.02 assessment, P05.03 budgets and ADR-016 saved-plan/field-ownership choice.

**Outputs:** Canonical plan schema, digest rules, effect graph, mappings and version/freshness binding.

1. Pin intent, policy, profiles, qualification, inventory generation and exact adapter/automation artifacts.
2. Specify execution/recovery boundaries and exclude secrets; distinguish volatile request metadata from canonical semantic inputs.

**Acceptance:** Equivalent pinned inputs yield equivalent canonical output; changed object, scope, artifact or policy invalidates the prior digest and approval.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G05.02. Reviewer: Architecture and governance owners.

### P05.05 — Review experience

**Owner:** Console. **Traceability:** R12 R33; G05.01 G05.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P05.01–P05.04 assessment/plan APIs and P02.04 approval contract.

**Outputs:** Destination comparison, unmet-requirement remediation, plan diff and approval handoff screens.

1. Show exact supported scope, conditional dependencies, destructive effects and bounded downtime assumptions.
2. Keep operator choices tied to plan revision and digest; handle stale views by refreshing and requiring renewed review.

**Acceptance:** An operator explains why a destination is blocked and what changed; changed plans cannot reuse a displayed prior approval.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G05.01 G05.02. Reviewer: Product owner and qualification lead.

### P05.06 — Admission contract

**Owner:** Planning/governance/lifecycle. **Traceability:** R14 R17; G05.02 G05.03 G05.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P02.04 bound approvals, P05.03 reservation design and P05.04 immutable plans; ADR-018 lane policy.

**Outputs:** Planning/governance/lifecycle admission contract and rejection fixtures.

1. Define the atomic admission record and exact freshness, entitlement, qualification and approval preconditions.
2. Specify invalidation/recheck boundaries and separate authorized isolated campaigns from operational support claims.

**Acceptance:** Revoked approval, expired facts, changed scope or missing exact-tuple qualification cannot admit operational execution; lab authority cannot target production.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G05.02 G05.03 G05.04. Reviewer: Governance, lifecycle and security owners.
