# G05 — Capabilities and immutable plans review

Review P05 against G05.01–G05.04 in the [gate specification](../../implementation/gates.md), using [P05 work packages](../../implementation/phases/p05.md). Planning/architecture owners review compilation, governance/security review authority, and lifecycle/quality review reservations.

## Assemble the review

1. Pin intent, observation generations, policies, capability profiles, qualification records, contract and service/artifact revisions for two authorized destinations.
2. Gather [Q03](../campaigns/q03-planning-and-authority.md) findings, canonical plans, approval invalidation, operational/lab lane denials and allocation race/fault reports.
3. Obtain lifecycle journal and authoritative allocation-owner observations; distinguish available capacity, reserved capacity and observed live consumption.

## Criterion examination

| Criterion | Review action |
| --- | --- |
| G05.01 | Reproduce destination findings from pinned inputs and inspect requirement-level eligible/blocked/unknown explanations, qualification scope and remediation. |
| G05.02 | Compare canonical plan digests, exact object mappings, artifacts, budgets, effect graph and recovery boundaries; verify each material change invalidates approval. |
| G05.03 | Remove/stale mandatory capability, tenant, sovereignty or exact-tuple evidence and confirm operational eligibility fails; check no unsupported route inference. |
| G05.04 | Inspect concurrent/partial/expired reservation cases through journal and owner receipts; confirm uncertain/live allocations are reconciled before release. |

## Resolve the review

An explainable feasibility candidate may be selected for a separately authorized laboratory campaign while normal operational admission remains blocked by missing qualification. Verify this separation without circularly requiring a new tuple to have already passed native qualification before its first lab test.

Reservation coordination cannot assume a global transaction across native owners. Accept only observed idempotent owner operations and explicit holds/compensation for partial outcomes; timer expiry alone must not release live consumption.

Record reproducible input and plan identities, criterion findings, evidence references, actual reviewers/date and required remediation. The [delivery register](../../implementation/delivery-register.yaml) owns the decision; passing G05 authorizes neither native effects nor a changed plan.

## Reopen when

Reevaluate affected checks after capability semantics, source freshness, plan canonicalization, authority binding, lab admission policy, allocation contracts or recovery-boundary changes.
