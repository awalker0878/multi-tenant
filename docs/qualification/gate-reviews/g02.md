# G02 — Identity, tenancy and governance review

Review P02 against G02.01–G02.04 in the [gate specification](../../implementation/gates.md), using [P02 work packages](../../implementation/phases/p02.md). IAM/security and governance coordinate with quality/product reviewers. Test evidence must cover both user and service callers across at least two tenants.

## Assemble the review

1. Pin console-managed OIDC connection and session/workload-trust revisions, role/delegation policy, approval semantics and service/contract revisions. Record bootstrap/change/retirement states without credentials; external OIDC settings must not come from deployment configuration.
2. Obtain the complete [Q01](../campaigns/q01-contract-and-tenancy.md) role/surface matrix, negative responses, audit history, identity-failure timelines and keyboard task observations.
3. For approval checks before the P05 producer exists, verify synthetic immutable plan fixtures conform to the agreed interface and are visibly distinguished from actual compiled plans.

## Criterion examination

| Criterion | Review action |
| --- | --- |
| G02.01 | Inspect Q01.17–Q01.19 bootstrap display/hash custody, forced password change, temporary-password rejection and console OIDC setup. Inspect tenant/caller/token/search/export/evidence denials through console and direct APIs. |
| G02.02 | Alter plan digest, action, scope and expiry; verify revocation and separation of duties prevent invalid admission and preserve attributable decisions. |
| G02.03 | Compare issuer/key loss, rotation and revocation with documented validity/cache bounds. Q01.20 must prove verified OIDC handover revokes local access and that outage/retry/restore cannot resurrect it; failed setup must preserve local administration. |
| G02.04 | Verify approval/audit history survives restart; observe delegated users completing tenant selection and denial journeys using keyboard controls. |

## Resolve the review

Require evidence for every exposed surface and authority boundary, not only middleware unit tests. Verify that a caller cannot bypass the console and assert a tenant, actor or approver identity that overrides authenticated authority.

Unexplained cross-tenant data, stale privileged admission or missing immutable history blocks the corresponding criterion. Register the affected surface and exact reproduction rather than accepting an aggregate test percentage.

Record actual reviewer/date, source/configuration revisions, evidence IDs, per-criterion assessment and remedial work in the protected review record; the [delivery register](../../implementation/delivery-register.yaml) owns the gate decision. Reexercise plan binding against the real producer during P05 integration.

## Reopen when

Review affected cases after new endpoints/projections/exports, session or issuer changes, delegation/approval policy changes, key-cache behavior changes or changes to how plan identity is bound.
