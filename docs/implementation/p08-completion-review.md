### First commissioning inputs

For each approved source and target environment, supply its tenant/site/environment
binding, approved API origin/trust reference, scoped secret-custody references and
the worker placement allowed to reach it. The source needs its vCenter scope;
the target needs its OpenStack project scope. Readers, writers and independent
observers require distinct identities and separately scoped grants. Provide the
custody references, not secret values. Installed versions, VM hardware, disks,
networks, target capabilities and quota facts must then be collected through APIs.

Select the first representative application and its authoritative consistency,
fencing, restore and recovery protocol. Its owner supplies correctness criteria,
outage/data-loss objectives, business-effect suppression and retention decisions.
Those choices determine the remaining guest/delta/service/recovery adapters and
the approved copy-only lab test. They cannot be inferred from a VM inventory.

Physical performance qualification must measure capture, transfer, conversion,
import, validation and cutover at each proposed concurrency on the exact route,
including shared datastore/network/worker contention and production impact. Publish
the retained results through the observation interface, then derive wave durations
and select real windows. The scheduler holds unmeasured or stale routes and never
uses an administrator-entered transfer rate as benchmark evidence.

## Native execution and receiving sequence

1. Use the delivered Inventory/Console review and Planning preparation to bind current
   profiles and complete datasets. Compose the complete native plan with the selected stage effects and independent
   readers against the supplied protocols; do not enable simulation owners for writes.
2. Verify G07 entry and the installed tuple, profile revisions, method, all disks/
   datasets and approved objectives. Complete Q07.01 denials for missing, stale or
   unsupported facts. Review converter/guest artifact and quarantine qualification.
3. Execute Q07.02–Q07.04 on an isolated representative workload: suppress business
   effects; capture/export/convert/import only the copy; retain native IDs, OVF,
   digests and interruption records. An interrupted transfer remains held; restarting
   the activity or deleting its output directory is not reconciliation.
4. Exercise Q07.05–Q07.06 with independent all-writer observations. Retain final
   synchronization before guest shutdown when the chosen delta needs a live guest.
   Record last source write, first possible target write and actual service outage.
5. Execute Q07.07 before target writes and Q07.08 after a known committed target
   record and attachment. Compare all data and relationships after recovery; prove
   target changes survive and the old source was not blindly restarted.
6. Complete Q07.09 service/policy/restore and objective measurements, and Q07.10
   source/key retention and premature-deletion denial. Cleanup has separate authority;
   migration success does not retire the source.
7. Apply the [G08 review procedure](../qualification/gate-reviews/g08.md). Record
   each actual reviewer, date, immutable source/tuple/artifact references, original
   observations, findings and decision. No automated test supplies that review.
