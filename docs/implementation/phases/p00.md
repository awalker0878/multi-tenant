# P00 detailed work packages

These cards define implementation work. P00 has started; the [baseline review](../p00-baseline-review.md) links actual review and spike outputs. The [decision/input review](../p00-decision-and-input-review.md) consolidates DC01–DC10 choices, IP01–IP07 required inputs and their existing blocking checkpoints. No card is complete merely because its document exists. Current state and actual evidence live in [the canonical register](../delivery-register.yaml). [Gate criteria](../gates.md) own pass/fail outcomes; [status rules](../status-model.md) own state transitions. Estimates are focused person-days per package, excluding approval/access lead time and parallel effort. They are planning inputs to refine in P00.06, not elapsed commitments. Output paths identify artifacts to create or refine. Existing documents are inputs to review; code, accepted decisions and measured evidence must still be delivered through the package.

The [accountable G00 decision](../../qualification/gate-reviews/g00-user-decision-2026-10-04.md) approves advancement to P01. Original task axes retain carried incomplete work; the decision assigns full application/configuration and candidate topology to P01.02/P01.06 and actual operating/native inputs to their existing checkpoints. The earlier engineering assessment and measured results remain unchanged.

## P00.01 — Scope and journeys

**Accountable role:** Product lead. **Contributors:** Architecture, application owner, service owner.

**Prerequisites:** Source direction and README; proposed walkthrough available. No lab access required.

**Traceability:** R01 R36. Decisions: ADR-001 ADR-014 ADR-021 ADR-022. Acceptance criterion: **G00.01**.

**Estimate and confidence:** 3–5 person-days; medium confidence.

Confirm personas and lifecycle outcomes with prospective owners. Freeze one synthetic multi-workload Linux application, source/target direction, data correctness/outage expectations, mandatory capability dimensions and explicit first-release exclusions. Decide whether retained operational state actually exists. Assign a real person to each accountable role without treating role names as acceptance.

**Outputs:** Existing README and `docs/product/application-walkthrough.md`; review `docs/product/scope.md` and the release-scope record in `docs/decisions/adr-NNN-short-title.md`.

**Acceptance checks:**

- Each persona has a task, permitted scope and observable success outcome.
- Every initial route/method/platform boundary is explicit; all deferred breadth remains visible.
- Retained-state applicability has a records-owner decision or is listed as an unresolved input.

**Required evidence:** Reviewed scope, owner roster and inclusion/exclusion decision (E0). Store immutable references in the register; review procedure and evidence bindings: `docs/qualification/gate-reviews/g00.md`.

**Reviewer:** Product and service owners; actual identities recorded at review.

**Dependency/blocker treatment:** Input needed: available product/application/service owners. Unblock: obtain scope and data-retention answers; continue domain/spike work meanwhile. Create a register blocker only when confirmed; include scope, owner, unblock condition and next action.

## P00.02 — Domain and ownership

**Accountable role:** Architecture lead. **Contributors:** Catalogue/governance/planning/lifecycle owners; security.

**Prerequisites:** P00.01 candidate scope; can overlap its review.

**Traceability:** R01 R06. Decisions: ADR-004 ADR-012 ADR-013 ADR-024. Acceptance criterion: **G00.02**.

**Estimate and confidence:** 4–7 person-days; medium confidence.

Review domain cardinalities and lifecycle invariants against the worked application. Assign one authoritative writer to intent, observation, assessment, plan, approval, admission, native operation, reservation journal and evidence. Resolve shared-domain and cross-tenant rules, revision checks and ownership transfer. Review the seven service specifications before public schemas are authored.

**Outputs:** Existing `docs/product/domain-model.md`, `docs/product/application-walkthrough.md`, `docs/services/*.md` and `docs/contracts/README.md`; planned resolved ADR-004/012/013 records.

**Acceptance checks:**

- Walk at least one multi-WSD/multi-domain example and invalid cross-tenant association through every owner.
- Distinguish workload identity, observed native identity and approved field/resource ownership.
- Every command/event example identifies writer, tenant/actor authority and concurrency rule; no cross-service private-table access is required.

**Required evidence:** Reviewed entity/invariant cases and context authority decisions (E0). Store immutable references in the register; review procedure and evidence bindings: `docs/qualification/gate-reviews/g00.md`.

**Reviewer:** Architecture lead and owning service reviewers; security reviews trust boundaries.

**Dependency/blocker treatment:** Input needed: domain/sharing decisions from service owners. Unblock: resolve disputed cardinality in ADR-013; do not bake an assumption into P03 schemas. Create a register blocker only when confirmed; include scope, owner, unblock condition and next action.

## P00.03 — Compatibility and runtime decisions

**Accountable role:** Engineering and SRE leads. **Contributors:** Architecture, IAM/security, frontend and Python engineers.

**Prerequisites:** P00.01 stack constraints; P00.02 candidate service boundaries.

**Traceability:** R01. Decisions: ADR-002 ADR-003 ADR-005 ADR-006 ADR-007 ADR-008 ADR-009 ADR-010 ADR-011 ADR-012 ADR-019 ADR-020. Acceptance criterion: **G00.03**.

**Estimate and confidence:** 5–10 person-days; low confidence until dependency/network access is known.

Run an isolated disposable compatibility spike for the requested PHP/frontend/Python stack. Resolve locks and image versions, build Inertia/Vue, typecheck TypeScript and execute minimal PHP/Python checks. Record supported runtime choices and select now-blocking infrastructure decisions with operating owners. Define initial identity/trust integration, contract generation and restricted-network dependency strategy; no product features are implemented by this spike.

**Outputs:** Implemented experimental `spikes/compatibility/` inputs and measured locks/results, indexed in [compatibility results](../p00-compatibility-results.md), [Python tooling](../p00-python-tooling-results.md), [Laravel integration](../p00-integration-results.md), [browser results](../p00-browser-results.md), [candidate image/BOM](../p00-image-results.md) and [contract tooling](../p00-contract-tooling-results.md); resolved `docs/decisions/adr-NNN-short-title.md`; update existing `docs/operations/deployment-model.md` with accepted selections.

The image and contract probes now have executable inputs and dedicated result records. Use those reports for actual run outcomes, retained failures and any outstanding execution; do not repeat their implementation as a future task. A measured candidate still needs the scoped runtime, distribution, trust, browser and contract-dialect decisions in DC04–DC08. Continue runnable experiments without waiting for those owners to accept a production baseline, and keep synthetic tool evidence separate from P01 service builds and real-dependency integration.

**Acceptance checks:**

- Record actual successful or failed commands, exact versions and artifact identities; a compatibility assertion without execution cannot pass.
- Build from locks in a clean environment and record unsupported combinations/browser constraints.
- NOW ADRs have accountable decisions; provisional choices identify assumptions, owner and final blocking package.

**Required evidence:** Dependency locks, build/typecheck/test outputs (E1), infrastructure and trust decisions (E0). Store immutable references in the register; review procedure and evidence bindings: `docs/qualification/gate-reviews/g00.md`.

**Reviewer:** Engineering/SRE leads and security for trust/custody choices.

**Dependency/blocker treatment:** Input needed: dependency access and supported enterprise runtime owners. Unblock: use approved mirrors or obtain the specific missing decision; preserve failures rather than claiming resolution. Create a register blocker only when confirmed; include scope, owner, unblock condition and next action.

## P00.04 — Route feasibility and qualification design

**Accountable role:** Qualification and platform leads. **Contributors:** Infrastructure, security, application/data and service owners.

**Prerequisites:** P00.01 candidate application; P00.02 authority model.

**Traceability:** R01. Decisions: ADR-014 ADR-015 ADR-018. Acceptance criterion: **G00.04**.

**Estimate and confidence:** 5–10 person-days plus access lead time; low confidence.

Select exact candidate VMware/OpenStack tuples, target image, application deployment/configuration artifacts, storage/network topology and stateful datasets. Assess the preferred `application_rebuild_restore` method against reproducibility, consistent capture, complete restore, source fencing, cutover and post-target-write recovery. Prepare positive/negative/recovery campaigns and a separate scoped lab authorization lane. Cold guest/disk conversion and import remains a separately selected and qualified option for P09; it is not equivalent evidence or an automatic fallback.

**Outputs:** Existing support and walkthrough specifications; reviewed `docs/qualification/feasibility/initial-route.md`, selected tuple record and `docs/qualification/campaigns/q07-first-offline-migration.md`.

**Acceptance checks:**

- Source/target/method/guest/data/service/topology tuple and fixture provenance are explicit.
- Actual bounded feasibility results identify constraints and unknowns; no simulation is labeled native support.
- Campaign design tests unfenced source denial and both pre-target-write rollback and post-target-write recovery.
- Lab authority and production admission cannot be confused; access prerequisites have owners and next actions.

**Required evidence:** Feasibility observations/spike results (E1/E2 where performed) and reviewed campaign/authority design (E0). Store immutable references in the register; review procedure and evidence bindings: `docs/qualification/gate-reviews/g00.md`.

**Reviewer:** Independent qualification reviewer, platform and application owners; security reviews lab scope.

**Dependency/blocker treatment:** Input needed: installed facts, permitted fixtures and lab owners. Unblock: obtain exact scoped access/data; contracts may proceed but G00.04 cannot pass on an untested route assertion. Create a register blocker only when confirmed; include scope, owner, unblock condition and next action.

## P00.05 — Operating and security requirements

**Accountable role:** SRE and security leads. **Contributors:** Product, application, records and service owners.

**Prerequisites:** P00.01 scope; P00.04 candidate route/topology.

**Traceability:** R01 R32 R34 R36. Decisions: ADR-010 ADR-011 ADR-017 ADR-020 ADR-021. Acceptance criterion: **G00.05**.

**Estimate and confidence:** 4–7 person-days; low confidence until owner input.

Set initial and growth workload tiers, API/endpoint budgets, SLO/latency/RPO/RTO and application outage/data objectives. Identify trust boundaries, permitted custody/location/support access, keys and sensitive evidence. Define retention, support responsibilities, alert delivery and recovery acceptance. Distinguish control-plane restore from application data recovery after target writes.

**Outputs:** Reviewed `docs/product/operating-targets.md`, `docs/operations/threat-model.md` and `docs/operations/runbooks/recovery.md`; update `docs/operations/deployment-model.md` and relevant ADRs.

**Acceptance checks:**

- Every target has units, scope, workload model, measurement method and accountable owner.
- Data/control/management flows identify allowed direction, identity, custody and failure behavior.
- Key/identity/site loss and restored stale workflow state have testable recovery/hold outcomes.
- Records owner decides retention/import applicability; support access and sovereignty are concrete deployment constraints.

**Required evidence:** Reviewed targets, trust/data flows, threat/recovery cases and retention decision (E0). Store immutable references in the register; review procedure and evidence bindings: `docs/qualification/gate-reviews/g00.md`.

**Reviewer:** Security, SRE, application and records owners for their scopes.

**Dependency/blocker treatment:** Input needed: service assurance classification, ownership and target values. Unblock: accountable owners ratify or replace candidate values; do not convert proposed numbers into promises. Create a register blocker only when confirmed; include scope, owner, unblock condition and next action.

- The six G00 criteria each have evidence or a concrete blocker; failed/unresolved criteria keep the gate open.

**Required evidence:** Traceability/backlog review (E0), actual gate decision and evidence references. Store immutable references in the register; review procedure and evidence bindings: `docs/qualification/gate-reviews/g00.md`.
