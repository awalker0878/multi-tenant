## BL-P05-001 — designated receiving review

| Reviewer role | Concrete record to examine | Decision to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planning and quality | Two authorized candidates, original owner inputs, requirement findings and remediation; actual P04 owner holds versus controlled positive E2 inputs. | G05.01 reproducibility and explanation. |
| Architecture and Governance | Canonical content/binding digests, exact mappings/artifacts, native-plan/state/sole-writer choice, changed-plan denial, effect graph and target-first-write boundary. | G05.02 integrity and ADR-016 receiving disposition. |
| Security and Assurance | Current source authority, tenant/custody/isolation denials, dossier custody and exact-scope matching, separate campaign endpoint/credential/data/cleanup limits. | G05.03 lane and safety acceptance. |
| Lifecycle and quality | Independent owner receipts, concurrent allocation, unknown outcomes, partial compensation and live-resource expiry hold. | G05.04 journal and failure acceptance. |

Record actual names, dates, immutable evidence, limits, defects and decisions.
The G05 criteria require E1/E2 integration/simulation; native E3 is not a circular
prerequisite for a new tuple's separately authorized first lab campaign. Missing
P04 native acceptance still prevents claims of operational support.

A representative operator and product/quality reviewer should perform:

1. Compare the two candidates and explain one mandatory blocker and its remedy.
2. Identify scope, expiry, destructiveness, downtime assumption and recovery boundary.
3. Compare a materially changed plan and explain why the prior approval cannot apply.
4. Handle an uncertain compile reply by retrying unchanged; confirm only one plan.
5. Observe qualification revocation and actor revocation; verify approval disables
   and protected content clears, including history navigation.

Use the selected managed browser/OS/policy and assistive combination from OP07.
Retain operator outcomes and resolve mandatory defects. Automated keyboard/reflow
checks supply bounded engineering evidence, not a representative user's result.

## P06 handoff

P06.01 can start from the owned persisted plan and journal contracts. Implement
atomic admission/command receipt/reservation binding/dispatch outbox, durable job
state, independently observed simulated effects and immediate effect rechecks.
Preserve stable command identity, unknown-outcome reconciliation and separate lab
scope. A plan, approval or timer never authorizes a native effect by itself.

Before native operations, the accountable owners must select and qualify actual
native API contracts, adapter artifacts and custody, compiler/adapter/automation images,
source policy and Assurance evidence custody, authenticated allocation owners,
endpoint/data scope, capacity/retention budgets and operating controls. These are
P06/P07 and carried P00/P04 inputs; the P05 synthetic packet appoints no people or
production endpoints and supplies no native support statement.
