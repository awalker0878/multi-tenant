# P09 — Platform and capability expansion

Expand only through named platform, method, guest and adoption tranches whose support claims have separate evidence.

**Accountable lead:** Infrastructure and qualification leads. Individual assignees and reviewers are recorded when assigned.

**Entry:** Reusable P05–P08 contracts and individual tranche readiness; native campaign authority per exact tuple.

**Sequence:** P09.01 freezes tranche scope; P09.02–P09.04 advance independently where prerequisites hold; P09.05 binds extension compatibility.

Use the [programme plan](../phased-plan.md), [canonical delivery register](../delivery-register.yaml), [requirements](../requirements-and-qualification.md) and [decision register](../../decisions/decision-register.md) together. The register owns current state and evidence; these cards define delivery scope. Refine staffing and estimates using the [estimation model](../estimation-and-dependencies.md) before starting each package.

## Work packages

### P09.01 — Platform tranches

**Owner:** Infrastructure. **Traceability:** R07 R08 R09 R20 R26; G09.01 G09.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** Reusable P05–P08 contracts; ADR-022 named tranche and platform/service-owner participation.

**Outputs:** Tranche capability baseline and VMware/AHV lifecycle adapters with explicit constraints.

1. Choose exact platform/backend/network/storage variants and included operations by business demand.
2. Implement adapter conformance and denied unsupported cases before separately authorized native campaigns.

**Acceptance:** Every advertised capability maps to the selected tranche tuple; contracts do not imply vendor-wide parity.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G09.01 G09.02. Reviewer: Product/architecture and platform owners.

### P09.02 — Migration matrix

**Owner:** Infrastructure/quality. **Traceability:** R26 R27; G09.01 G09.02.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P09.01 tranche scope, P08 recovery contracts and guest/data/method feasibility.

**Outputs:** Directed migration matrix and per-method Linux/Windows/appliance qualification plans.

1. Represent six cross-platform directions separately, alongside selected same-family topologies.
2. Pin disk/boot/driver/encryption/data requirements and measure recovery for each advertised direction and method.

**Acceptance:** No reverse-route, guest or method inherits native acceptance; unsupported variants remain visible with their blockers.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G09.01 G09.02. Reviewer: Platform/security and qualification owners.

### P09.03 — Brownfield adoption

**Owner:** Inventory/lifecycle. **Traceability:** R11; G09.03.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P04 ownership observations, P06 field-authority protocol and explicit owner transfer agreement.

**Outputs:** Observation-to-adoption workflow with no-change import, drift review and detach/relinquish behavior.

1. Enumerate exact object/field scope and old writers; compare proposed import/state against independent native observations.
2. Prove no-change reconciliation before separate authority transfer; contain collisions/drift and support controlled detach.

**Acceptance:** Import alone grants no write authority; old writer transfer is verified and uncertain ownership blocks mutation.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G09.03. Reviewer: Inventory/lifecycle and independent platform observer.

### P09.04 — Enterprise capabilities

**Owner:** Planning/workers. **Traceability:** R07 R20 R27 R28; G09.02 G09.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P09.01 tranche capabilities, agreed shared risk budgets and selected HA/day-two operating requirements.

**Outputs:** Selected HA, policy change, patch/rotation, scaling and enterprise scheduling capabilities.

1. Declare each operation and topology change with safety, service and recovery prerequisites.
2. Schedule by dependency DAG, window and tenant/endpoint budgets; exercise pause/stop and correlated-failure scenarios.

**Acceptance:** Concurrent impact stays within accepted budgets; failover preserves mandatory policy and an explicit stop boundary is observable.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G09.02 G09.04. Reviewer: SRE/security and qualification leads.

### P09.05 — Extension contract

**Owner:** Architecture. **Traceability:** R09 R13 R26 R27 R31; G09.01 G09.04.

**Dependencies and inputs:** P09.01–P09.04 extension needs and ADR-012/022 compatibility and support policy.

**Outputs:** Signed adapter package contract, conformance kit, compatibility rules and operator extension guide.

1. Bind declared capability/constraint versions to signed artifacts and owning interfaces.
2. Test supported upgrades/recovery and rerun affected common paths before publishing changed support claims.

**Acceptance:** Incompatible or untrusted adapters fail admission; support metadata names exact version, expiry and retest triggers.

**Evidence and review:** Record artifact/source identities, actual observations and failed cases at the levels specified by G09.01 G09.04. Reviewer: Architecture/security and assurance owners.
